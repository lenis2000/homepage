These notes accompany two overview lectures given at the conference [Solvable lattice models, representation theory of quantum groups and algebraic combinatorics](https://www.mittag-leffler.se/activities/solvable-lattice-models-representation-theory-of-quantum-groups-and-algebraic-combinatorics/) at Institut Mittag-Leffler, Djursholm, Sweden, July 27-31, 2026.

The goal is to present the Yang-Baxter equation as a source of Markov operators which transport probability measures attached to vertex models, via local Yang-Baxter moves. When both sides of the equation are sums of nonnegative weights, one can randomize the identity into a Markov step turning a configuration on the left into a randomly chosen configuration on the right; carried across the lattice, these steps become a random growth process. This passage from an identity to a randomized transformation is the *bijectivization*, or *probabilistic bijection*.

The notes recall the vertex models whose partition functions are the Schur symmetric polynomials, prove the Cauchy identity from the Yang-Baxter equation, and then derive the classical Robinson-Schensted-Knuth correspondence directly from the Yang-Baxter equation for the monochrome Schur vertex model. Optional sections describe the $q$- and $t$-deformations leading to $q$-Whittaker and Hall-Littlewood polynomials, fusion, and the particle systems ($q$-PushTASEP, the stochastic six-vertex model) obtained from the deformed weights. A final section points to further directions: stationary measures, symmetries in the spectral parameters, shift invariance, and Markov duality.

These are notes in progress, and comments are welcome.
