{% raw %}<p>In this paper we study the oriented swap process on the positive integers and its asymptotic properties. Our results extend a theorem by Angel, Holroyd, and Romik regarding the trajectories of particles in the finite oriented swap process. Furthermore, we study the evolution of the type of a particle at the leftmost position over time. Our approach relies on a relationship between multi-species particle systems and Hecke algebras, complemented by a detailed asymptotic analysis.</p>{% endraw %}
