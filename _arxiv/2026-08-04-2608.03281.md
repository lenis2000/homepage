{% raw %}<p>We study partitions which attain the largest Littlewood-Richardson coefficient. More precisely, we prove that the largest <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msubsup><mi>c</mi><mrow><mi>μ</mi><mi>ν</mi></mrow><mi>λ</mi></msubsup></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">c^\lambda_{\mu \nu}</annotation></semantics></math></span> is attained at partitions such that <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>μ</mi><mo>⊆</mo><mi>ν</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\mu \subseteq \nu</annotation></semantics></math></span> and <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>ν</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mi>μ</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\nu/\mu</annotation></semantics></math></span> is a disjoint union of squares. We conjecture that for <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi><mo>≥</mo><mn>16</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n \ge 16</annotation></semantics></math></span>, all largest <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msubsup><mi>c</mi><mrow><mi>μ</mi><mi>ν</mi></mrow><mi>λ</mi></msubsup></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">c^\lambda_{\mu \nu}</annotation></semantics></math></span> must satisfy this property. We confirm this conjecture numerically, for <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>16</mn><mo>≤</mo><mi>n</mi><mo>≤</mo><mn>45</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">16 \le n \le 45</annotation></semantics></math></span>.</p>{% endraw %}
