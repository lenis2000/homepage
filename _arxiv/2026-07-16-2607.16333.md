We then develop a graph-theoretic approach to the singularity and stability problem for non-symmetric matrices <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>S</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">S</annotation></semantics></math></span>.
