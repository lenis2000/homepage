{% raw %}<p>We establish the relation between two objects: an integrable system related to Painlev\'e II equation, and the symplectic invariants of a certain plane curve S(TW). This curve describes the average eigenvalue density of a random hermitian matrix spectrum near a hard edge (a bound for its maximal eigenvalue). This shows that the s -> -infinity asymptotic expansion of Tracy-Widow law F_{GUE}(s), governing the distribution of the maximal eigenvalue in hermitian random matrices, is given by symplectic invariants.</p>{% endraw %}
