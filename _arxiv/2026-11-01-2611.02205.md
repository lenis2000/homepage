{% raw %}<p>We determine the joint moments of real powers of the absolute value of characteristic polynomials in the circular beta ensemble, for every <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>β</mi><mo>&gt;</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\beta&gt;0</annotation></semantics></math></span>. At fixed distinct prescribed points, the Fisher--Hartwig formula holds for every exponent greater than <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mo>−</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">-1</annotation></semantics></math></span>, with explicit constants and a relative error bounded by a negative power of the matrix size <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N</annotation></semantics></math></span>. For approaching points in the same integrable range, the error is bounded by a negative power of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi><msub><mi>δ</mi><mi>N</mi></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N\delta_N</annotation></semantics></math></span>, provided that <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi><msub><mi>δ</mi><mi>N</mi></msub><mo>→</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">∞</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N\delta_N\to\infty</annotation></semantics></math></span> and the sum of the absolute values of the negative exponents is less than two. Here <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>δ</mi><mi>N</mi></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\delta_N</annotation></semantics></math></span> is the minimum distance between the prescribed points; this range includes every integrable pair of root exponents. For <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi><mo>&gt;</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q&gt;0</annotation></semantics></math></span> at the critical relation <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>p</mi><msup><mi>q</mi><mn>2</mn></msup><mo>=</mo><mn>2</mn><mi>β</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">pq^2=2\beta</annotation></semantics></math></span>, we prove the Keating--Wong asymptotic for the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>p</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">p</annotation></semantics></math></span>th moment of the integral of the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q</annotation></semantics></math></span>th absolute power of the characteristic polynomial, for every real <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>p</mi><mo>&gt;</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">p&gt;1</annotation></semantics></math></span>. The leading asymptotic extends to every complex spatial weight in <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msup><mi>L</mi><mi>p</mi></msup></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">L^p</annotation></semantics></math></span>. The proofs combine the recursion for orthogonal polynomials on the unit circle with conditional expectation identities for Gaussian multiplicative chaos and quantitative estimates for approaching singularities.</p>{% endraw %}
