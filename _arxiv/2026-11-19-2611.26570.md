{% raw %}<p>The completely packed <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>O</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mn>1</mn><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">O(1)</annotation></semantics></math></span> loop model can be viewed as an interface representation of critical bond percolation on the square lattice. We consider submatching events, which prescribe how these interfaces pair finitely many consecutive boundary points. Romik's rationality conjectures predict that the probabilities of these events on the semi-infinite cylinder are given by specific rational functions, which even become dyadic rationals in the half-plane limit. We derive explicit Pfaffian formulae for the probability of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>k</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">k</annotation></semantics></math></span> nested arches on the semi-infinite cylinder of circumference <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>2</mn><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">2N</annotation></semantics></math></span>, valid for <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>k</mi><mo>&lt;</mo><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">k&lt;N</annotation></semantics></math></span>. We deduce that these probabilities are rational functions of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N</annotation></semantics></math></span> and, by taking the limit <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi><mo>→</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">∞</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N\to\infty</annotation></semantics></math></span>, that the corresponding half-plane probabilities remain rational. This establishes key aspects of Romik's conjectures. Our proof uses identities of Fonseca and Zinn-Justin, derived through the qKZ formalism, which express these probabilities as ratios of enumeration numbers for totally symmetric self-complementary plane partitions (TSSCPPs). These plane partitions admit a dimer representation, allowing us to apply Kasteleyn theory. We evaluate the resulting hypergeometric sum using Zeilberger's and Gosper's algorithms and then take the half-plane limit <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi><mo>→</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">∞</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N\to\infty</annotation></semantics></math></span>. Along the way, we derive a somewhat simplified expression for the inverse Kasteleyn matrix entries (encoding correlations in TSSCPPs) of Ayyer and Chhita, which might be of independent interest.</p>{% endraw %}
