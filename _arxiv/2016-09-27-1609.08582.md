{% raw %}<p>For the two-dimensional one-component Coulomb plasma, we derive an asymptotic expansion of the free energy up to order <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N</annotation></semantics></math></span>, the number of particles of the gas, with an effective error bound <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msup><mi>N</mi><mrow><mn>1</mn><mo>−</mo><mi>κ</mi></mrow></msup></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N^{1-\kappa}</annotation></semantics></math></span> for some constant <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>κ</mi><mo>&gt;</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\kappa &gt; 0</annotation></semantics></math></span>. This expansion is based on approximating the Coulomb gas by a quasi-free Yukawa gas. Further, we prove that the fluctuations of the linear statistics are given by a Gaussian free field at any positive temperature. Our proof of this central limit theorem uses a loop equation for the Coulomb gas, the free energy asymptotics, and rigidity bounds on the local density fluctuations of the Coulomb gas, which we obtained in a previous paper.</p>{% endraw %}
