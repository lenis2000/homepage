{% raw %}<p>We investigate the size of the convoy in the speed process in the multi-species asymmetric simple exclusion process (ASEP). Through a coupling argument, we obtain an exact formula for the expected convoy size by relating it to a combinatorial structure. We prove that the asymptotic expected convoy size is universal for all fixed jump rates <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi><mo>∈</mo><mo stretchy="false">[</mo><mn>0</mn><mo separator="true">,</mo><mn>1</mn><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q \in [0,1)</annotation></semantics></math></span>. In the special case <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q=0</annotation></semantics></math></span>, we upgrade this to full convergence in distribution. We further establish a critical scaling <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>1</mn><mo>−</mo><mi>γ</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><msqrt><mi>n</mi></msqrt></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q = 1 - \gamma/\sqrt{n}</annotation></semantics></math></span> that yields a nontrivial limiting regime. Our analysis builds on Martin's construction of the convoy and makes use of an orthogonal-polynomial representation of random-walk transition probabilities.</p>{% endraw %}
