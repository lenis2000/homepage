{% raw %}<p>This paper establishes a mesoscopic central limit theorem for linear statistics of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>β</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\beta</annotation></semantics></math></span>-ensembles or log-gas, as the dimension <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi><mo>→</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">∞</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N\to\infty</annotation></semantics></math></span>, in the temperature regime <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>1</mn><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mi>N</mi><mo>≪</mo><mi>β</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>N</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mo>≤</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">1/N\ll\beta(N)\le 1</annotation></semantics></math></span>. For simplicity, we assume that the potential is one-cut regular and analytic. In this regime, the size of the fluctuations depends on <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>β</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>N</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\beta(N)</annotation></semantics></math></span> and the mesoscopic scale. We show that there is a transition at a critical <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>η</mi><mo>≍</mo><mn>1</mn><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mi>N</mi><mi>β</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>N</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\eta \asymp 1/ N\beta(N)</annotation></semantics></math></span> between a Random Matrix regime, where the limiting variance is given by the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msup><mi mathvariant="sans-serif">H</mi><mrow><mn>1</mn><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mn>2</mn></mrow></msup></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\mathsf{H}^{1/2}</annotation></semantics></math></span>-norm and a Poisson regime where the limiting variance is given by the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msup><mi>L</mi><mn>2</mn></msup></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">L^2</annotation></semantics></math></span>-norm. We also describe the critical regime. The proof of the CLT relies on optimal local laws at intermediate temperatures and Stein's method for <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>β</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\beta</annotation></semantics></math></span>-ensembles. In particular, in this regime, it is necessary to construct new correction terms to the classical equilibrium measure to obtain a suitable re-centring of linear statistics and describe their fluctuations. We also obtain a free energy expansion.</p>{% endraw %}
