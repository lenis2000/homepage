{% raw %}<p>We give an algebraic branching program of polynomial size which computes Cayley determinant of right quantum matrices. This is a rare example of an efficient computation of a noncommutative determinant, and the first such example for quantum groups. We extend the results to the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q</annotation></semantics></math></span>-Cayley determinant of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q</annotation></semantics></math></span>-right quantum matrices, as well as to their multiparameter generalization. The proofs are entirely combinatorial, as we relate Cayley, Moore and Valiant determinants using bijections/involutions on words. We then employ the celebrated determinant construction of Mahajan and Vinay (SODA'97), to obtain the results.</p>{% endraw %}
