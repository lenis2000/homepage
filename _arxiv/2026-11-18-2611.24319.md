{% raw %}<p>We determine the diagonal shape function of lattice last passage percolation up to dimension-dependent constants and characterize its finiteness by a tail integral. The proof transfers estimates from an entropy-controlled Poisson model whose quadratic penalty makes the curvature dependence explicit. For critical tails of exponent <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q</annotation></semantics></math></span> in dimension <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi><mo>≥</mo><mn>2</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q\geq 2</annotation></semantics></math></span>, the mean passage time has order <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>log</mi><mo>⁡</mo><mi>n</mi><msup><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mrow><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>q</mi><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mn>2</mn><mi>q</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow></msup></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n(\log n)^{(q+1)/(2q)}</annotation></semantics></math></span>, proving sharpness of the logarithmic exponent in the lower bound of Ganguly, Ginsburg and Nam.</p>{% endraw %}
