{% raw %}<p>We prove a Central Limit Theorem for the empirical measure in the one-dimensional Totally Asymmetric Zero-Range Process in the hyperbolic scaling <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N</annotation></semantics></math></span>, starting from the equilibrium measure <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>ν</mi><mi>ρ</mi></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\nu_{\rho}</annotation></semantics></math></span>. We also show that when taking the direction of the characteristics, the limit density fluctuation field does not evolve in time until <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msup><mi>N</mi><mrow><mn>4</mn><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mn>3</mn></mrow></msup></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N^{4/3}</annotation></semantics></math></span>, which implies the current across the characteristics to vanish in this longer time scale.</p>{% endraw %}
