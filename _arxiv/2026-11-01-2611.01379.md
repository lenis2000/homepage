{% raw %}<p>The main result of this paper is an upper bound on the correlations of multiplicative functionals of determinantal point processes. In particular, for the sine process, the correlations of a multiplicative functional associated with a rapidly decaying function decay quadratically. It follows that the spectral density of such functionals belongs to the Sobolev space <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>H</mi><mn>1</mn></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">H_1</annotation></semantics></math></span>.</p>{% endraw %}
