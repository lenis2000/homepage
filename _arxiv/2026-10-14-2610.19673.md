{% raw %}<p>We study random walks with geometric or log-gamma increments, reweighted by certain exponential functionals. We establish precise asymptotics for all moments of exponential functionals, revealing a phase transition as the drifts of the walks vary. To describe the large time limit of reweighted random walks, we introduce a discrete <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>T</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">T</annotation></semantics></math></span> transformation on random walk paths and establish Girsanov relations. As a corollary, we show that the stationary measures of geometric last passage percolation and the log-gamma polymer on a strip converge to their half-space counterparts as the strip width tends to infinity. In the log-gamma case, this provides an alternative proof of a recent result in arXiv:2607.17030.</p>{% endraw %}
