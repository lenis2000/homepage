{% raw %}<p>We prove Krattenthaler's conjecture from 2001 about the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>1</mn><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mn>3</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">1/3</annotation></semantics></math></span>-phenomenon for lozenge tilings of semiregular hexagons. In a first step we reduce the problem to the case of regular hexagons. In a second step we further reduce the question to a special case already covered in the literature. This is achieved by vast application of the celebrated Zeilberger Algorithm and the Holonomic Ansatz.</p>{% endraw %}
