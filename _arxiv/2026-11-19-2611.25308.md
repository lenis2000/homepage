{% raw %}<p>We consider the Cauchy problem for the defocusing Hirota equation with non-zero boundary conditions \begin{align*} &iq_t+ q_{xx}-2\left(|q|^2-1\right)q +i\left(q_{xxx}-6|q|^2q_x\right)=0,\\ &q(x,0)=q_0(x)\to\pm 1, \quad x\to\pm\infty, \end{align*} which can be characterized using a Riemann-Hilbert problem through the inverse scattering transform. With the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mover accent="true"><mi mathvariant="normal">∂</mi><mo>ˉ</mo></mover></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\bar{\partial}</annotation></semantics></math></span>-steepest descent method and double scaling limit technique, we derive the long-time asymptotics of the solution in two transition regions \begin{align*} \mathcal{P}_{a} :=\left\{(x,t)\in\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{+}: \left| {x}/{t}-a\right|t^{2/3}<C\right\}, \quad a\in\{-4,-8\}, \end{align*} where <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>C</mi><mo>&gt;</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">C&gt;0</annotation></semantics></math></span> is a constant. It is shown that the asymptotic expansions in two transition regions can be expressed in terms of the Hastings--McLeod solution of the Painlevé II equation in the generic case and Ablowitz--Segur solutions in the non-generic case.</p>{% endraw %}
