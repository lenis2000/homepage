{% raw %}<p>We compute the dimension of the center of the Temperley--Lieb algebra <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal"><mi mathvariant="normal">T</mi><msub><mi mathvariant="normal">L</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">n</mi></msub></mi><mo>⁡</mo><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>δ</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\operatorname{TL_n}(\delta)</annotation></semantics></math></span> over a field of characteristic zero for every nonzero value of the parameter <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>δ</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\delta</annotation></semantics></math></span>. The proof uses the cellular filtration by cup number, together with known facts about the representation theory of the Temperley--Lieb algebra, especially the structure of its standard modules and their radicals. Dilation and compression maps compare the induced graded pieces of the center at levels <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n</annotation></semantics></math></span> and <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi><mo>−</mo><mn>2</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n-2</annotation></semantics></math></span>, giving an upper bound of one for each such piece. A deformation argument gives the matching lower bound, and hence <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>dim</mi><mo>⁡</mo><mi>Z</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><msub><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">TL</mi><mo>⁡</mo></mrow><mi>n</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>δ</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mo>=</mo><mn>1</mn><mo>+</mo><mo fence="true" stretchy="true" minsize="1.8em" maxsize="1.8em">⌊</mo><mfrac><mi>n</mi><mn>2</mn></mfrac><mo fence="true" stretchy="true" minsize="1.8em" maxsize="1.8em">⌋</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex"> \dim Z(\operatorname{TL}_n(\delta))=1+\Bigl\lfloor \frac{n}{2}\Bigr\rfloor</annotation></semantics></math></span>. We also prove that every central element is fixed by the canonical anti-automorphism and by the natural diagram-reflection automorphism. Finally, we give a congruence criterion for the trivial-radical case and record a Gram-matrix computation for leading terms.</p>{% endraw %}
