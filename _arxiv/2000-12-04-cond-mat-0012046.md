{% raw %}<p>Exact integral expressions of the skew orthogonal polynomials involved in Orthogonal (beta=1) and Symplectic (beta=4) random matrix ensembles are obtained: the (even rank) skew orthogonal polynomials are average characteristic polynomials of random matrices. From there, asymptotics of the skew orthogonal polynomials are derived.</p>{% endraw %}
