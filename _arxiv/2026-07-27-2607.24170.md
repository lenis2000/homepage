{% raw %}<p>We construct a finite-energy Wigner counterexample and a concrete one-parameter family of Gaussian-regularised Bernoulli entry laws yielding counterexamples to the conjectural dimensional monotonicity of the quadratically penalised logarithmic energy for mean empirical spectral distributions by Chafaï, Dadoun, and Youssef.</p>{% endraw %}
