{% raw %}<p>Every finite-length path in the directed landscape spends almost all of its time on geodesics. More precisely, let G be the union of the interiors of all geodesics. We prove that, almost surely, every path of finite landscape length lies in G outside a set of times of Hausdorff dimension at most 57/61. We also construct finite-length paths whose exceptional set has dimension at least 1/3. The proof combines quantitative bounds on nearly optimal detours, stochastic domination of the environment outside a geodesic, and a three-geodesic disjointness estimate.</p>{% endraw %}
