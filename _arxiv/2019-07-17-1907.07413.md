The Marcenko--Pastur (MP) density <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>ρ</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>x</mi><mo separator="true">;</mo><mi>r</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>x</mi><mo>≥</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\rho(x; r), x \geq 0</annotation></semantics></math></span> describes the distribution for squares of the singular values of the random matrices in this ensemble in the scaling limit <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi><mo>→</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">∞</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N \to \infty</annotation></semantics></math></span>, <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>M</mi><mo>→</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">∞</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">M \to \infty</annotation></semantics></math></span> with a fixed rectangularity <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>r</mi><mo>=</mo><mi>N</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mi>M</mi><mo>∈</mo><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mn>0</mn><mo separator="true">,</mo><mn>1</mn><mo stretchy="false">]</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">r=N/M \in (0, 1]</annotation></semantics></math></span>. The dynamical extension of the squared-singular-value distribution is realized by the noncolliding squared Bessel process, and its hydrodynamic limit provides the two-parametric MP density <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>ρ</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>x</mi><mo separator="true">;</mo><mi>r</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>t</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\rho(x; r, t)</annotation></semantics></math></span> with time <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>t</mi><mo>≥</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">t \geq 0</annotation></semantics></math></span>, whose initial distribution is <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>δ</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>x</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\delta(x)</annotation></semantics></math></span>. Recently, Blaizot, Nowak, and Warchol studied the time-dependent complex Wishart ensemble with an external source and introduced the three-parametric MP density <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>ρ</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>x</mi><mo separator="true">;</mo><mi>r</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>t</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>a</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\rho(x; r, t, a)</annotation></semantics></math></span> by analyzing the hydrodynamic limit of the process starting from <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>δ</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>x</mi><mo>−</mo><mi>a</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>a</mi><mo>&gt;</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\delta(x-a), a &gt; 0</annotation></semantics></math></span>. In the present paper, we give useful expressions for <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>ρ</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>x</mi><mo separator="true">;</mo><mi>r</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>t</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>a</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\rho(x; r, t, a)</annotation></semantics></math></span> and perform a systematic study of dynamic critical phenomena observed at the critical time <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>t</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">c</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>a</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mo>=</mo><mi>a</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">t_{\rm c}(a)=a</annotation></semantics></math></span> when <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>r</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">r=1</annotation></semantics></math></span>. The universal behavior in the long-term limit <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>t</mi><mo>→</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">∞</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">t \to \infty</annotation></semantics></math></span> is also reported.
