Our main result describes its fluctuations near the boundary of the region affected by the conditioning. More precisely, in the spatial window <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>x</mi><mo>=</mo><mi>t</mi><msqrt><mi>L</mi></msqrt><mo>+</mo><mi>O</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mn>1</mn><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">x=t\sqrt L+O(1)</annotation></semantics></math></span>, the centered field converges in finite-dimensional distributions to the KPZ fixed point with a half-Brownian initial profile. The convergence holds jointly at arbitrary positive times, including times before and after the conditioning time. The proof is based on an asymptotic correspondence between conditional narrow-wedge multipoint formulas and those for the half-Brownian KPZ fixed point.</p>{% endraw %}
