{% raw %}<p>In this paper we construct a one-parameter family of periodic Airy line ensembles <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msup><mi mathvariant="script">A</mi><mrow><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">p</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">e</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">r</mi></mrow><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>R</mi></mrow></msup></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{per},R}</annotation></semantics></math></span> for <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>R</mi><mo>&gt;</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">R&gt;0</annotation></semantics></math></span> as weak scaling limits of Borodin's periodic Schur processes. We establish several properties of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msup><mi mathvariant="script">A</mi><mrow><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">p</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">e</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">r</mi></mrow><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>R</mi></mrow></msup></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{per},R}</annotation></semantics></math></span>, including a determinantal point process structure with an explicit correlation kernel. We also show that, after a parabolic shift and normalization, <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msup><mi mathvariant="script">A</mi><mrow><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">p</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">e</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">r</mi></mrow><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>R</mi></mrow></msup></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{per},R}</annotation></semantics></math></span> satisfies, on its domain <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mo stretchy="false">[</mo><mo>−</mo><mi>R</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mn>2</mn><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>R</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mn>2</mn><mo stretchy="false">]</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">[-R/2,R/2]</annotation></semantics></math></span>, the same Brownian Gibbs property enjoyed by the usual Airy line ensemble. A main technical contribution of the paper is a general tightness criterion for determinantal line ensembles, which extends the argument used by Johansson in [Comm. Math. Phys. 242 (2003), 277-329] to prove a functional limit theorem for the discrete polynuclear growth model.</p>{% endraw %}
