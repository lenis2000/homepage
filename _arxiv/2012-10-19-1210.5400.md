{% raw %}<p>We compute analytically the probability density function (pdf) of the largest eigenvalue <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>λ</mi><mi>max</mi><mo>⁡</mo></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\lambda_{\max}</annotation></semantics></math></span> in rotationally invariant Cauchy ensembles of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi><mo>×</mo><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N\times N</annotation></semantics></math></span> matrices. We consider unitary (<span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>β</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>2</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\beta = 2</annotation></semantics></math></span>), orthogonal (<span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>β</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\beta =1</annotation></semantics></math></span>) and symplectic (<span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>β</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>4</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\beta=4</annotation></semantics></math></span>) ensembles of such heavy-tailed random matrices. We show that a central non-Gaussian regime for <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>λ</mi><mi>max</mi><mo>⁡</mo></msub><mo>∼</mo><mi mathvariant="script">O</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>N</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\lambda_{\max} \sim \mathcal{O}(N)</annotation></semantics></math></span> is flanked by large deviation tails on both sides which we compute here exactly for any value of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>β</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\beta</annotation></semantics></math></span>. By matching these tails with the central regime, we obtain the exact leading asymptotic behaviors of the pdf in the central regime, which generalizes the Tracy-Widom distribution known for Gaussian ensembles, both at small and large arguments and for any <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>β</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\beta</annotation></semantics></math></span>. Our analytical results are confirmed by numerical simulations.</p>{% endraw %}
