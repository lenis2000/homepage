We study here truncated linear statistics where the sum is restricted to the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>N</mi><mn>1</mn></msub><mo>&lt;</mo><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N_1&lt;N</annotation></semantics></math></span> largest eigenvalues: <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mover accent="true"><mi>L</mi><mo>~</mo></mover><mo>=</mo><msubsup><mo>∑</mo><mrow><mi>i</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><msub><mi>N</mi><mn>1</mn></msub></msubsup><mi>f</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><msub><mi>λ</mi><mi>i</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\tilde{L}=\sum_{i=1}^{N_1}f(\lambda_i)</annotation></semantics></math></span>. Motivated by the analysis of the statistical physics of fluctuating one-dimensional interfaces, we consider the case of the Laguerre ensemble of random matrices with <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>λ</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mo>=</mo><msqrt><mi>λ</mi></msqrt></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f(\lambda)=\sqrt{\lambda}</annotation></semantics></math></span>. Using the Coulomb gas technique, we study the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi><mo>→</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">∞</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N\to\infty</annotation></semantics></math></span> limit with <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>N</mi><mn>1</mn></msub><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N_1/N</annotation></semantics></math></span> fixed. In this latter case the density presents a logarithmic divergence inside the bulk.
