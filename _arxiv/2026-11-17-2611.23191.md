{% raw %}<p>We study the asymptotic behavior of a second class particle in ASEP on the integer lattice. We prove a general formula for the distribution of its position at any fixed time for arbitrary initial conditions. As a first application, we obtain its limiting distribution for arbitrary initial conditions in the rarefaction fan case, generalizing the result of Cator--Pimentel from TASEP to ASEP.</p>{% endraw %}
