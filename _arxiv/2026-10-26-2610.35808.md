{% raw %}<p>We establish operator universality at the spectral edge of general Wigner matrices by showing that their suitably rescaled Lanczos tridiagonalizations, constructed from deterministic initial vectors, converge to the stochastic Airy operator with Dirichlet boundary condition. The result requires control of the entire tridiagonal structure on the scale relevant to the limiting differential operator. The Lanczos coefficients for non-Gaussian Wigner matrices are strongly dependent. We analyze their fluctuations by combining multi-resolvent local laws with quantitative control of Krylov subspaces and a comparison of the resulting Jacobi coefficients with those of the Gaussian ensembles. This approach identifies the limiting Brownian potential directly from the original matrix entries, without invoking Dyson Brownian motion in the step of Green function comparison. The resolvent limit yields joint convergence of edge eigenvalues, embedded eigenfunctions, and spectral measures.
