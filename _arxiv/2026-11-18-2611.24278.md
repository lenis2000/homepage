{% raw %}<p>We study a random lozenge tiling model of a regular hexagon with weights that are doubly periodic with period <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>3</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">3</annotation></semantics></math></span>. This model features a smooth phase with six cusp points. We obtain explicit descriptions of the limit shape and the local scaling limits of the correlation kernel. Both limits are expressed as contour integrals on the Harnack curve that underlies the model, or on its double cover that is obtained by gluing together two copies along the support of an equilibrium measure in an external field. Our asymptotic analysis is based on the double contour integral representation of the correlation kernel due to Duits and Kuijlaars. This double contour integral contains a factor that is given in terms of the solution of a Riemann-Hilbert problem for matrix-valued orthogonal polynomials. The asymptotic behavior of this Riemann-Hilbert problem was recently studied by one of the authors using the Deift-Zhou method of steepest descent. In this work, we complement this approach with the asymptotic analysis of the double contour integral. The integrals are moved to the double cover of the Harnack curve. We analyze the effect of the first two transformations of the Deift-Zhou method, and deform the contours to the relevant saddle points. We visualize our asymptotic analysis on the amoeba of the Harnack curve, in the spirit of the work of Berggren and Borodin on the doubly periodic Aztec diamond.</p>{% endraw %}
