{% raw %}<p>We study the local eigenvalue correlations of products of independent induced symplectic Ginibre matrices in the large-dimensional limit. We establish the scaling limits of the correlation functions in the bulk, at the spectral edges, and outside the limiting spectral support, both near and away from the real axis. At regular spectral points, we show that the limiting correlations coincide with those of a single symplectic Ginibre ensemble, exhibiting Pfaffian structures near the real axis and determinantal structures away from it. At the origin, where the limiting spectral density develops a singularity, we derive an integral representation of the parameter-dependent limiting Pfaffian prekernel. Our proofs are based on a unified approach involving a differential equation for the skew prekernels and their asymptotics.</p>{% endraw %}
