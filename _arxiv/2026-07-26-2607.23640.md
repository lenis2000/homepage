{% raw %}<p>We study the non-equilibrium density fluctuations of a one-dimensional two-species symmetric simple exclusion process in contact with critical slow boundary reservoirs. The boundary rates are of order <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>1</mn><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mi>n</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">1/n</annotation></semantics></math></span>, which leads to Robin boundary conditions at the macroscopic level. We prove that the coupled fluctuation field associated with the empirical densities of two species converges to a generalized Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process. The limiting process is characterized by a linear martingale problem whose coefficients reflect the combined effects of bulk diffusion, species conversion, and the boundary reservoirs.</p>{% endraw %}
