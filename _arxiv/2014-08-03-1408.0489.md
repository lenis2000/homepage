{% raw %}<p>In these notes we give a simple proof of the second-order Boltzmann-Gibbs Principle, which is the main tool in order to prove that the equilibrium fluctuations of the WASEP are given, in the regime of the critical strength asymmetry, by the Stochastic Burgers equation.</p>{% endraw %}
