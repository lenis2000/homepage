{% raw %}<p>We study correlation functions in the model of coalescing and annihilating random walks on the one-dimensional periodic lattice <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi mathvariant="double-struck">Z</mi><mi>N</mi></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\mathbb{Z}_N</annotation></semantics></math></span>. We prove that, for a special class of initial conditions, the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n</annotation></semantics></math></span>-point correlation function can be represented as a linear combination of two Pfaffians of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>2</mn><mi>n</mi><mo>×</mo><mn>2</mn><mi>n</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">2n\times 2n</annotation></semantics></math></span> matrices constructed from <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>2</mn><mo>×</mo><mn>2</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">2\times2</annotation></semantics></math></span> matrix kernels, which in turn can be expressed in terms of two scalar kernels. This is an analogue of the single-Pfaffian form exhibited by correlation functions of the same model on <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi mathvariant="double-struck">Z</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\mathbb{Z}</annotation></semantics></math></span>, where it serves as a signature of a Pfaffian point process. We derive equations for the kernels and construct their solutions. We also show that a particular example of an initial condition leading to bi-Pfaffian correlation functions is the inhomogeneous Bernoulli point process, which includes a fixed initial configuration as a particular limiting case.</p>{% endraw %}
