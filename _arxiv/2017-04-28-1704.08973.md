{% raw %}<p>We consider a one-dimensional classical Coulomb gas of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N</annotation></semantics></math></span> like-charges in a harmonic potential -- also known as the one-dimensional one-component plasma (1dOCP). We compute analytically the probability distribution of the position <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>x</mi><mi>max</mi><mo>⁡</mo></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">x_{\max}</annotation></semantics></math></span> of the rightmost charge in the limit of large <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N</annotation></semantics></math></span>. We show that the typical fluctuations of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>x</mi><mi>max</mi><mo>⁡</mo></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">x_{\max}</annotation></semantics></math></span> around its mean are described by a non-trivial scaling function, with asymmetric tails. This distribution is different from the Tracy-Widom distribution of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>x</mi><mi>max</mi><mo>⁡</mo></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">x_{\max}</annotation></semantics></math></span> for the Dyson's log-gas. We also compute the large deviation functions of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>x</mi><mi>max</mi><mo>⁡</mo></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">x_{\max}</annotation></semantics></math></span> explicitly and show that the system exhibits a third-order phase transition, as in the log-gas. Our theoretical predictions are verified numerically.</p>{% endraw %}
