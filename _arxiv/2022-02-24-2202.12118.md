We study two observables: (i) the distribution of the gap between two consecutive particles in the bulk and (ii) the distribution of the number of particles <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>N</mi><mi>I</mi></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N_I</annotation></semantics></math></span> in a fixed interval <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>I</mi><mo>=</mo><mo stretchy="false">[</mo><mo>−</mo><mi>L</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mo>+</mo><mi>L</mi><mo stretchy="false">]</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">I=[-L,+L]</annotation></semantics></math></span> inside the bulk, the so-called full-counting-statistics (FCS).
