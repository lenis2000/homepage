{% raw %}<p>We prove that broad families of regular pattern packings are enumerated by moments of probability measures on <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mo stretchy="false">[</mo><mn>0</mn><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">∞</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">[0,\infty)</annotation></semantics></math></span>. A packing prescribes occurrences of a consecutive permutation pattern at equally spaced positions, with successive occurrences overlapping and together covering the permutation. This regularity underlies the structure theory for the associated pattern-packing posets that we developed in Part I (arXiv:2610.29846). We obtain moment representations for all two-level shifted-grid families of Part I and all overlap-one patterns whose first and last letters differ by one or two. For families whose posets are trees with a unique minimum or maximum and overlap at most half the pattern length, positivity instead holds after finitely many initial terms of the enumerating sequence have been discarded. Our proofs develop further enumeration formulas and combine probabilistic constructions with total nonnegativity and weighted paths, yielding explicit laws or continued fractions in several cases. Although a previously conjectured universal moment property fails, our results establish positivity for all packing sequences associated with patterns of length at most four and all but one of the distinct sequences arising from patterns of length five. Extensive numerical evidence suggests that the remaining sequence is also a moment sequence. These results lead us to formulate refined conjectures for all overlap-one patterns and for the shifted-grid families of Part I with arbitrarily many levels.</p>{% endraw %}
