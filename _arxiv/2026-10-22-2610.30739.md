{% raw %}<p>We derive exact formulas for the diagonal distribution of half-space geometric last-passage percolation with a two-parameter family of stationary initial conditions throughout the phase diagram. Unlike previous results, our formulas remove the random shift at the origin. Under critical scaling, we obtain new formulas for the half-space stationary KPZ fixed-point distribution at <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>x</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">x=0</annotation></semantics></math></span> in the high-density and maximal-current phases. In the low-density phase, we derive a new representation and show that it matches the corresponding formula from the stationary log-gamma polymer.</p>{% endraw %}
