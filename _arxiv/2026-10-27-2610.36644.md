{% raw %}<p>We describe two-parametric Littlewood identities for inhomogeneous stable spin Hall-Littlewood and inhomogeneous spin <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q</annotation></semantics></math></span>-Whittaker symmetric functions. These identities generalize the Littlewood identity found by Warnaar (2006) for the ordinary Hall-Littlewood polynomials. However, instead of Rogers-Szegö coefficients used by Warnaar, coefficients in our identities are given by products of Al-Salam-Chihara polynomials. Our proof relies on integrable lattice models. A key step includes three new solutions to the reflection and boundary equations for the fully fused six-vertex model. To find these solutions we use isomorphisms between tensor products of evaluation <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>U</mi><mi>q</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>L</mi><msub><mrow><mi mathvariant="fraktur">s</mi><mi mathvariant="fraktur">l</mi></mrow><mn>2</mn></msub><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">U_q(L\mathfrak{sl}_2)</annotation></semantics></math></span>-modules, as well as covectors stable under a <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q</annotation></semantics></math></span>-Onsager coideal subalgebra.</p>{% endraw %}
