{% raw %}<p>We show that every stationary solution of the Dobrushin--Lanford--Ruelle (DLR) equations for the one-dimensional log-gas with a quantitative control on its number variance minimizes the free energy functional. This yields a DLR variational principle for this continuous long-range system. Moreover, we show that any limit point of the finite-volume log-gases is stationary and satisfies this condition. This allows us to give a new, physical proof of the existence of a microscopic limit for the Gaussian Beta Ensembles, known as the Sine-beta point process.</p>{% endraw %}
