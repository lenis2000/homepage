Exact Sampling of q-Racah Tilings by Shuffling

Markov operators changing the hexagon [Borodin–Gorin 2008], [Borodin–Gorin–Rains 2009]: explicit kernels mapping the \(q\)-Racah measure on the \((T,S,N)\) hexagon to the one on \((T,S\pm1,N)\)
Algorithm:
  • \(S=0\): a parallelogram, unique tiling
  • \(S\to S+1\): update the paths slice by slice, \(t=1,\ldots,T\): forced moves, plus random splits of blocks of adjacent particles with explicit probabilities
  • Each step is exact; cost \(O(NT)\) per step, \(O(NTS)\) in total
Code: Gorin's 2007 Fortran program, ported to Python and to C++/WebAssembly, which generates the exact \(q\)-Racah samples live in this browser
