Barcode Kernel: Closed Form

Theorem [Knizel–P., in preparation]
Write \(w:=\chi^2\) and \(x_s:=\frac{1-s}{2}\). The regularized series of the conjecture converges for all \(s,t\in\mathbb{Z}\), and for \(r=s-t\ge 1\)
\(\displaystyle \mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{barcode}}(s,t)=\frac{(-1)^{r+1}\,P_{s,t}}{q^{-r}-1}\Bigl(c^{(0)}_{s,t}+q^{-r/2}\,c^{(1)}_{s,t}\Bigr),\qquad c^{(p)}_{s,t}:=B\,G_p(x_s)\,G_p(x_{t-1}),\)
with \(P_{s,t}\) the prefactor of the conjecture, \(B=\dfrac{\theta_{\sqrt q}(\chi)^2}{w\,\theta_q(-1/w)}\), \(\theta_p(z)=(z;p)_\infty(p/z;p)_\infty\), and the vertex functions
\(\displaystyle G_0(x)=C(x)\,\theta_{q^2}\bigl(q^{2x+1}w\bigr),\quad G_1(x)=C(x)\,\chi\, q^{x+\frac14}\,\theta_{q^2}\bigl(q^{2x+2}w\bigr),\quad C(x)=\sqrt{1+\tfrac{q^{-2x}}{w}}\;\frac{(q^2;q^2)_\infty}{(q;q)_\infty^2\,\theta_{\sqrt q}\bigl(\chi q^{x+\frac12}\bigr)}.\)
Why the series collapses
  • Exact all-orders expansion of the eigenfunctions: \(\mathcal{F}_n(x)=\pm\,G_p(x)\,\Phi_p\bigl(q^{n+1};x\bigr)\), \(p=2n \bmod 2\), with \(\Phi_p(\,\cdot\,;x)\) an entire theta series, \(\Phi_p(0;x)=1\)
  • \(G_0,G_1\) are the two zero modes of the one-body operator (eigenvalues \(-q^n\to 0\))
  • The renormalizer \(q^{(M+1)(s-t)}\) keeps only the leading term \(\Phi_p(0;x)=1\)
Consequences
  • An elementary quotient of Jacobi theta functions; reproduces the numerics of [Knizel–P. 2025]
  • Two-periodic: \(\mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{barcode}}(s+2,t+2)=\mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{barcode}}(s,t)\)
  • Rank-two integrable: for \(s\ne t\),
    \(\mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{barcode}}(s,t)=\dfrac{f_0(s)g_0(t)+f_1(s)g_1(t)}{q^{-(s-t)/2}-q^{(s-t)/2}}\)
  • Exponential decay: \(|\mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{barcode}}(s,t)|\le C\,q^{|s-t|/4}\), sharp
Settles the existence of the limit in the conjecture. That this kernel is the correlation kernel of the barcode process of the tiling is work in progress.
