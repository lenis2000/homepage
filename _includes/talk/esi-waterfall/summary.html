Summary

  • Universality is well-developed, including local translation-invariant Gibbs measures, GFF global fluctuations (also Airy/KPZ at the edge, and several other universal regimes)
  • The waterfall [Knizel–P. 2025]: at fixed \(q\), the q-Racah hexagon develops a region where the two-dimensional Gibbs structure collapses to a one-dimensional random interface (law of large numbers, exponential concentration)
  • Factorial Schur processes give a Schur-type route to the waterfall, with bulk, edge and corner limits [Li, in preparation]
  • Exact samplers (shuffling, Yang–Baxter) make the waterfall visible; for Glauber dynamics and CFTP the waterfall is a bottleneck in practice
