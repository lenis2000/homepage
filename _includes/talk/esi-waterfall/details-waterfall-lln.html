Waterfall Law of Large Numbers [Knizel-P. 2025]

Scaling: \(T = \lfloor L\mathsf{T}\rfloor,\ S = \lfloor L\mathsf{S}\rfloor,\ N = \lfloor L\mathsf{N}\rfloor\),   \(L\to+\infty\); \(q\in(0,1)\) and \(\chi>0\) fixed. Right: \(\mathsf{T}:\mathsf{S}:\mathsf{N} = 4:3:2\), and a sample with \(q = 0.9\), \(\chi = 1\)
Waterfall region (scaled coordinates \((\mathsf{t},\mathsf{x})\) of the paths):
\(\displaystyle \mathcal{W} = \bigl\{\mathsf{t}_l < \mathsf{t} < \mathsf{t}_r,\ \ |2\mathsf{x} - \mathsf{S} - \mathsf{t}| < \mathsf{N}\bigr\},\)
\(\mathsf{t}_l = |\mathsf{N}-\mathsf{S}|,\qquad \mathsf{t}_r = \min(\mathsf{N}+\mathsf{S},\, 2\mathsf{T}-\mathsf{N}-\mathsf{S})\)
Band of width \(\mathsf{N}\) around the center line \(\mathsf{x} = \tfrac12(\mathsf{t}+\mathsf{S})\); nonempty iff \(\mathsf{N} < \mathsf{T}\)
Theorem [Knizel-P. 2025] The kernel \(K_t\) of the particles (non-horizontal lozenges) on a vertical slice satisfies, for fixed \(\Delta x, \Delta y \in \mathbb{Z}\):
\[\lim_{L\to+\infty} K_{\lfloor L\mathsf{t}\rfloor}\bigl(\lfloor L\mathsf{x}\rfloor + \Delta x,\, \lfloor L\mathsf{x}\rfloor + \Delta y\bigr) = \left\{\begin{array}{ll} \mathbf{1}_{\Delta x = \Delta y}, & (\mathsf{t},\mathsf{x}) \in \mathcal{W};\\[2pt] 0, & (\mathsf{t},\mathsf{x}) \in \mathcal{W}^{\pm}, \end{array}\right.\]
where \(\mathcal{W}^{\pm}\) are the frozen regions above and below \(\mathcal{W}\). Moreover, a horizontal lozenge inside \(\mathcal{W}\) has probability \(< e^{-cL}\).
Limiting profile: paths have slope \(\tfrac12\) inside \(\mathcal{W}\), slope \(0\) or \(1\) outside, no fluctuations. Only a one-dimensional random interface (the barcode) survives in \(\mathcal{W}\).
