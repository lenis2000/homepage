Yang–Baxter Sampler for Factorial Schur Processes

Start from the other side of Cauchy: with all \(x\)-rows below all \(w\)-rows, the vertex model has a single, frozen configuration
Swap rows \(x_i \leftrightarrow w_j\) by the Yang–Baxter equation, bijectivised [Bufetov–P. 2019], [Bufetov–Mucciconi–P. 2021]: the \(R\)-vertex moves through the two rows column by column; at column \(k\), a Bernoulli choice
\(\displaystyle \frac{x_i+y_k}{w_j+y_k}\quad\text{vs.}\quad\frac{w_j-x_i}{w_j+y_k}\)
Each swap maps the Gibbs measure for one row order to the one for the swapped order ⇒ exact sample after \(NM\) swaps, no mixing time
