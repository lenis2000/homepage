Barcode Kernel: Elliptic Level

Two-periodic \(\Rightarrow\) block-Toeplitz, with \(2\times2\) matrix symbol \(\displaystyle \widehat{\mathcal{K}}(\theta)=\sum_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}\bigl[\mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{barcode}}(a+2m,b)\bigr]_{a,b\in\{0,1\}}\,e^{im\theta}\), in the variable \(\eta:=-q^{3/2}e^{i\theta}\).
Theorem [Knizel–P., in preparation]
  • \(\widehat{\mathcal{K}}\) is meromorphic in \(\eta\) and \(\widehat{\mathcal{K}}(q^2\eta)=D\,\widehat{\mathcal{K}}(\eta)\,D^{-1}\), \(D=\mathrm{diag}(q,1)\): a section of \(\mathrm{End}(\mathcal{O}_E\oplus\mathcal{L}_q)\) over the elliptic curve \(E=\mathbb{C}^\times/q^{2\mathbb{Z}}\), with \(\mathcal{L}_q\) a two-torsion line bundle
  • \(\operatorname{tr}\widehat{\mathcal{K}}\equiv 1\), and if \(\rho=1\), then \(\displaystyle \widehat{\mathcal{K}}_{00}(\eta)=\frac12\Bigl(1-\frac{R(w)}{R(\eta/q)}\Bigr)\), \(\displaystyle R(x):=\frac{\theta_q(x)}{\theta_q(-x)}\)
  • \(\widehat{\mathcal{K}}(\theta)\) is a rank-one orthogonal projection for every \(\theta\) \(\iff\) \(\rho(q)=1\)
Levels of translation-invariant determinantal kernels (by their symbols)
rational discrete sine kernel; incomplete beta kernel [Okounkov–Reshetikhin 2003]
trigonometric finite-temperature sine kernel [Moshe–Neuberger–Shapiro 1994], [Johansson 2007]
elliptic elliptic tail kernel [Cuenca–Gorin–Olshanski 2021]: from \(U(\infty)\), curve \(\mathbb{C}^\times/q^{\mathbb{Z}}\), free twist
barcode kernel: from random lozenge tilings, curve \(\mathbb{C}^\times/q^{2\mathbb{Z}}\), rigid two-torsion twist, \(d_0\ne d_1\)
Open: gap probabilities are block-Toeplitz determinants with an elliptic matrix symbol; their asymptotics call for an elliptic Szegő–Widom theory.
