Edge Waterfall and the Essential Corner [Li, in prep.]

Edge waterfall on \(z=t+K\), \(0<t<1\): same residue kernel, with the theta function replaced by a one-sided product
\(\displaystyle H^{\mathrm{edge}}_{\Delta t}(U)=\Big(\frac{\beta q}{U};q\Big)_{\Delta t}\Big(\frac{U}{\beta};q\Big)_{\infty}\,\Theta_q\Big(-\frac{U}{\alpha}\Big)\)
still two phases \(\theta\in\{0,1\}\), but no translation invariance
Essential corner \((t,z)=(0,K)\): full sequence \(M\to\infty\), limit independent of \(K\):
\[\mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{corner}}(T_1,\Delta z_1;T_2,\Delta z_2)=\frac{1}{(2\pi\mathrm{i})^2}\oint\!\!\oint\frac{dU\,dV}{V-U}\,\frac{H^{\mathrm{corner}}_{T_2}(U)}{H^{\mathrm{corner}}_{T_1}(V)}\,\frac{U^{r_2-1}}{V^{r_1}}\]
\(\displaystyle H^{\mathrm{corner}}_T(U)=\Big(\frac{\beta q}{U};q\Big)_T\Big(-\frac{\alpha q}{U};q\Big)_\infty\)
Match with [Borodin-Gorin 2013] (particle-hole duality): holes of the corner process \(=\) particles of the Markov process of infinitely many nonintersecting Bernoulli walks with \(\xi_i=q^{1-i}\), \(g_t(w)=1+\frac{\beta}{\alpha}q^t w\), started densely packed
  • evolves in the vertical direction \(T\); each particle stays or jumps by 1, only finitely many stay
  • explicit infinite-particle transition probabilities via the Feller property
